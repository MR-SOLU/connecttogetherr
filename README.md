# ConnectTogether Community

An interest-based social and team-building platform designed to connect like-minded people without interest mismatches. Whether you want to form a local sports squad, plan a group trip, share ideas, or discuss specific topics, ConnectTogether matches users into dedicated domain rooms to chat, collaborate, and build teams.

## Key Features
- Interest Domain Selection: Choose your specific domain (such as Sports, Travel, and more) from a structured dropdown so every conversation stays relevant to your interests.
- Context-Tagged Messaging: Tag messages by intent—such as Team, Plan, Question, or Mood/Discussion—so members immediately know what you want to talk about or organize.
- 1-on-1 & Group Collaboration: Connect with an individual or a larger group to brainstorm ideas, coordinate schedules, and form teams.
- Live Community Metrics: Displays real-time platform activity, including total people connected and teams successfully formed.
- Verified & Secure Access: Built-in authentication and role-based access controls to prevent fake accounts and keep rooms, teams, and member profiles secure.

## Tech Stack
- Frontend: React, TypeScript, TanStack Start, Tailwind CSS
- Backend & Database: Supabase, Drizzle ORM
